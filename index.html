I am on the 2026–2027 Economics Job Market

I am a PhD candidate in Economics in the IDEA Graduate Program at the Universitat Autònoma de Barcelona (UAB) and Barcelona School of Economics. My advisors are Joan Llull and Lídia Farré.

My research is in Labor Economics, using structural microeconometric methods to study labor market choices, wages, and inequality. My job market paper examines how workers' and managers' gender-role attitudes shape occupational sorting and gender wage inequality.
